Business question

Which products, channels, stores and customer groups drive revenue and profitability, and where can NaijaMart improve repeat purchase and operational performance?

End-to-end workflow

Excel: Audited 3,318 raw order rows, removed 18 duplicates, and retained 3,300 distinct orders.

PostgreSQL: Modelled five related datasets; used joins, CTEs, aggregation and window functions for business analysis.

Reporting: Designed a four-page Power BI reporting structure and an HTML dashboard reference. A native .pbix is not included.

03 / Customer insights & retention

Repeat purchasing

Of 922 purchasing customers, 741 made at least two completed orders during 2025 (80.4%).

Recency opportunity

558 customers had no recorded purchase in the final 60 days of 2025. This is an inactivity indicator, not a proven churn classification.

04 / Profitability & business opportunities

Discount exposure

₦5.78M in applied discounts (about 5.4% effective discount rate) warrants analysis of contribution margins and promotions.

Order friction

8.9% of unique orders were cancelled or returned. Investigate causes by store and channel before prescribing process changes.

Recommendations

Protect margin

Review low-margin staples, supplier costs and price/discount tests while tracking units and gross profit.

Improve retention

Segment customers by purchase recency and frequency, then test second-purchase and re-engagement programmes.

Reduce friction

Study return/cancellation patterns by store and channel and measure improvement against the same baselines.

Project scope: This is an educational portfolio project using simulated Nigerian retail transactions for January–December 2025. Gross profit is not net operating profit.
